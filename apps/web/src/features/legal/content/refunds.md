# Refund policy

This policy covers paid Jumper plans sold by {{operator}}. It sits alongside the [terms of service](/terms).

## Trying before you pay

Every account starts on the free plan, so you can try Jumper with real KiCad projects before paying anything.

## New subscriptions: 14 days

If you are unhappy with a new paid plan, write to {{email}} within **14 days of your first payment** for a full refund. No reason needed.

## Renewals

- **Monthly plans:** renewals are not refunded. Cancel any time; you keep the plan until the end of the period you paid for.
- **Annual plans:** if you cancel within 30 days of a renewal, we refund the unused months. After that, the plan runs to the end of the year.

## Changing plans

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately. You pay only the difference for the rest of the period.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current period. Nothing is lost: repositories over the new plan's limits become read-only until you are back within them.

## When we owe you a refund

You get a pro-rated refund for time you paid for but could not use if:

- we end your account without a breach of the terms on your part;
- we remove a paid feature you relied on;
- the service has a major outage that we do not fix within a reasonable time.

## Where the law says more

Nothing here reduces rights you have under consumer law where you live. For example, EU and UK consumers have a 14-day right to withdraw from online purchases.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 10 business days. Invoice customers are refunded by bank transfer or credit note.
