# Privacy policy

This policy explains what {{operator}} ("we") collects when you use Jumper at {{url}}, why, and the choices you have. We are the data controller. Contact: {{email}}.

## What we collect

| Data | Where it comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Username, email, display name, password hash | You, when you sign up | To run your account and sign you in |
| GitHub user ID and avatar URL | GitHub, if you sign in with it | To link your accounts |
| Repositories, issues, comments, reviews | You and your collaborators | It is the service you asked for |
| Results generated from your files (renders, BOMs, check reports, fab packages) | Our workers | To show you your design |
| Sessions: browser user agent, IP address, time | Your browser | To keep you signed in and let you see and end your sessions |
| Security log: sign-ins, token and permission changes, with IP address | Our servers | To protect your account and investigate abuse |
| Emails we send (verification, password reset, notifications) | Our servers | To deliver messages you asked for |

We do **not** use advertising, third-party analytics or tracking pixels, and we do not sell personal data.

## Cookies and local storage

- `jumper_session`: keeps you signed in. Strictly necessary; httpOnly.
- `oauth_state`: protects GitHub sign-in against forgery. Lasts 10 minutes.
- Local storage `jumper-theme`: remembers light or dark mode. Never leaves your browser.

No consent banner is needed because nothing here is used for tracking.

## Legal bases (GDPR)

- **Contract:** your account, repositories and everything the service does with them.
- **Legitimate interests:** security logs, abuse prevention, backups.
- **Consent:** optional emails such as digests, which you can switch off at any time.

## Who we share data with

- **Collaborators and the public:** what you put in a repository is visible to whoever can access it. Public repositories and public profiles are visible to everyone.
- **Infrastructure providers** that host our servers, storage and email, under contracts that require them to protect it.
- **Distributors** (LCSC, JLCPCB, Mouser, DigiKey) receive part numbers, keywords and quantities when you price a BOM. They receive no personal data.
- **Webhook endpoints** you configure receive the event payloads you chose.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it. Where we are allowed to, we tell you first.

## Retention

- Account data is kept while your account exists.
- When you delete your account, your repositories and personal data are removed straight away. Your comments and reviews in other people's repositories stay, credited to a placeholder "ghost" user.
- Backups expire within 35 days.
- Sessions expire automatically. Security logs are kept for 12 months.

## Your rights

You can see and correct your data under **Settings**, download it all under **Settings → Account → Export data**, and delete your account there too. You can also ask us at {{email}} for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to processing. You may complain to your local data-protection authority.

## Transfers

If data leaves the country where you live, we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

## Children

Jumper is not intended for children under 16.

## Changes

We will post changes here and, if they are significant, notify you by email or in the app.
